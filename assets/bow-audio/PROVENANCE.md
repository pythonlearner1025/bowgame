# Recorded bow release and arrow pass

The earlier synthesized release/flight sounds are replaced by two actual author-published recordings. Primary sources and licenses are in [LICENSES.md](LICENSES.md); machine-readable hashes and exact processing parameters are in [PROVENANCE.json](PROVENANCE.json).

`release-recorded.wav` uses source seconds **0.205–0.430** from Ali_6868's bow-release recording. Its isolated transient occurs around 0.22–0.27 seconds. Processing: mono 48 kHz, second-order 75 Hz high-pass and 4.2 kHz low-pass, gentle tanh transient rounding with knee 0.38, fixed gain 0.72, 8 ms attack and 55 ms decay fades. Result: 0.225 seconds, peak 0.1673, RMS 0.02728 before game mixing.

`whizz-recorded.wav` uses source seconds **0.110–0.245** from saturdaysoundguy's recorded arrow release and flyby. The source has a strong initial transient around 0.055 seconds and another transient near 0.36 seconds. The chosen middle interval retains the softer airborne pulse around 0.14–0.17 seconds and excludes both distinct transients. Identifying that middle pulse as the pass is an inference from the author's description and waveform/spectrum, not a claim of subjective audition or perfect acoustic separation. Processing: mono 48 kHz, second-order 350 Hz high-pass and 4.5 kHz low-pass, gentle tanh rounding with knee 0.08, unity gain, 18 ms attack and 35 ms decay fades. Result: 0.135 seconds, peak 0.03562, RMS 0.007587 before game mixing.

There is no peak or loudness normalization. The quiet natural source level is retained, particularly for the flyby. Runtime playback separately applies distance, route gain and the existing master limiter. Bow draw/fully-held silence and body/head impact sounds are unchanged by these assets.

Public MP3 previews were downloaded lawfully from the media URLs embedded in the author pages. The original source downloads and full HTML snapshots remain in `/tmp/kite3d-real-bow-audio`; their hashes are recorded. The original master WAVs require login and were not accessed. `mvp-demo/reference/recorded-bow-audio/prepare.py` reproduces the conversion from those downloaded previews. [Processing waveforms](processing-waveforms.png) show the selected source intervals and final envelopes. The agent cannot receive audio input, so final perceived quality remains subject to the user's audition.

Final runtime route gains are 0.8 for release and at most 0.9 for a near-miss whizz, further attenuated by pass distance. This gives pre-spatial/master RMS approximately 0.02182 and 0.006828 respectively. Compared with the immediately preceding procedural source levels (0.1300 release and 0.1607 whizz), these route-adjusted recordings are approximately 15.5 dB and 27.4 dB quieter. The release route plays one recorded voice; the previous extra launch swish is removed.
