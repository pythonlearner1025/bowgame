Both recordings are released by their authors under **CC0 1.0 Universal**: <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>. They may be copied, modified and redistributed, including commercially. Attribution is not required by CC0; the following credits preserve provenance.

- **Bow Release (Bow and Arrow)** — **Ali_6868**, March 20, 2017. [Original author page](https://freesound.org/people/Ali_6868/sounds/384915/). Used for `release-recorded.wav`.
- **Arrow Loose and Flyby** — **saturdaysoundguy**, May 28, 2017. [Original author page](https://freesound.org/people/saturdaysoundguy/sounds/394180/). Used for `whizz-recorded.wav`.

The bundled WAVs are modified excerpts from the public high-quality MP3 previews linked by those author pages. They were trimmed, filtered, softened and faded. The login-only original WAV downloads were not accessed. No Rust or other copyrighted game audio was extracted.

The original author pages identify both works as recordings. The flyby author specifically describes an arrow passing the microphone and identifies the recorder and microphone as a Zoom F4 and Rode NTG-3. `PROVENANCE.json` records source URLs, preview URLs, SHA-256 hashes, crop coordinates and processing parameters.
